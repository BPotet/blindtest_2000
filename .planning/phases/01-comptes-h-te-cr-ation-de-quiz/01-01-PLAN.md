## Phase Goal (MVP user story)

**As a** hôte de blindtest, **I want to** créer un compte, me connecter, et fabriquer un quiz complet avec de vrais clips audio extraits de YouTube (ou uploadés en secours) et des QCM, **so that** tout mon contenu est préparé et testé à l'avance, avant la soirée.

> Sourced from the ROADMAP Phase 1 `**Goal:**` line. The ROADMAP goal is written as French prose, not in the canonical `As a / I want to / so that` syntax; the three slots above are a formatting transform of that exact sentence (actor `un hôte`, capability `créer un compte … fabriquer un quiz complet`, outcome drawn from the project's standing decision that extraction happens at authoring time and never live). No new scope was invented. Surfaced for the developer in the planner return.
